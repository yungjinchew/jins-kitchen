Workflow Notes

Critical path: The stew governs service timing: wing browning → reduction → first oven phase → thigh braise → final seasoning. Bread and salad yield to the stew; do not delay stew service for either component.

Oven sequencing: Cake bakes before dinner workflow at 350°F / 177°C. Stew owns the oven at 325°F / 163°C. Begin raising toward 400°F / 205°C during the stew’s final 15 minutes; the stabilized braise tolerates this drift. Bread enters only after the oven confirms 400°F / 205°C.

Fennel, Celery, and Herb Salad

Yield: 6–8 · Total: 20 min · Active: 20 min

A crisp, acidic raw salad that cuts through the stew’s collagen-rich body.

Ingredients

IngredientAmount
Fennel bulbs, shaved thin2
Celery stalks, thinly sliced3
Parsley leaves½ cup
Lemon juice2 tbsp
Extra-virgin olive oil60 ml / ¼ cup
Diamond Crystal kosher saltto taste
Black pepperto taste
Shaved Parmesan, optional30 g / 1 oz

Prep

  1. Shave fennel thinly and slice celery thinly.
  2. Refrigerate vegetables until service.

Active Cooking

  1. Whisk lemon juice, olive oil, salt, and pepper. A lightly broken vinaigrette is intentional.

Holding & Assembly

  1. Dress salad immediately before serving.
  2. Toss with parsley.
  3. Add Parmesan only at final toss if using.

Critical Control Points

SeverityFailure Mode & Sensory CueRecovery
MajorDressing too early — vegetables soften and release water.Unrecoverable for salad texture; salad remains serviceable.

Adjust: Increase lemon slightly if stew reads especially rich; adjust salt after Parmesan if using.

Toasted Country Sourdough

Yield: 6–8 · Total: 15 min · Active: 5 min · Oven: 400°F / 205°C

Ingredients

IngredientAmount
Country sourdough loaf1 large
Olive oilfor brushing
Flaky saltto finish

Prep

  1. Slice bread thickly.

Active Cooking

  1. Raise oven to 400°F / 205°C and wait for full temperature recovery.
  2. Brush bread lightly with olive oil.
  3. Toast 8–10 minutes. Edges crisp and browned; center yields slightly under firm pressure.

Holding & Assembly

  1. Hold on rack up to 15 minutes.
  2. If crust softens, re-toast 3–4 minutes.

Critical Control Points

SeverityFailure Mode & Sensory CueRecovery
MinorOvertoasting — rigid center and dark edges.Partially recoverable with extra olive oil.

Chicken Stew with White Wine, Wings, and Gremolata

Yield: 6–8 · Total: ~2 hr 45 min · Active: ~50 min · Oven: 325°F / 163°C

A deep chicken stew with wing collagen, staged reduction, aromatic lift, split carrot timing, and fresh gremolata.

Ingredients

IngredientAmount
Stew
Boneless skinless chicken thighs, halved2 lb / 900 g
Chicken wings, separated1 lb / 450 g
Bacon, chopped3 slices
Onion, finely chopped1 medium
Celery rib, minced1
Garlic, minced2 cloves
Anchovy paste2 tsp
Dried thyme½ tsp
Bay leaf1
Parsley stemsfrom 1 bunch
Lemon peel strip1 thin strip
Chicken broth5 cups / 1.2 L
Dry white wine1 cup / 240 ml
Soy sauce1 tbsp
Unsalted butter57 g / 4 tbsp
All-purpose flour2½ tbsp
Small red potatoes, unpeeled, quartered1 lb / 450 g
Carrots, peeled, ½-inch pieces, divided4
Diamond Crystal kosher salt¾ tsp + to taste
Black pepperto taste
Finish & Gremolata
Sherry vinegar1–2 tsp
Parsley leaves, chopped2 tbsp
Lemon zest½ tsp
Garlic, microplaned¼ small clove

Prep

  1. Heat oven to 325°F / 163°C.
  2. Pat wings dry thoroughly.
  3. Season thighs with ¾ tsp Diamond Crystal kosher salt and black pepper; temper 15–20 minutes.
  4. Divide carrots into two equal portions.

Active Cooking

Critical path: wing browning → reduction → first oven phase → thigh braise. Do not shorten reduction.

Phase 1 — Render Bacon and Brown Wings

  1. Cook bacon in Dutch oven over medium-low heat until browned, 6–8 minutes; transfer to bowl.
  2. Increase heat to medium. Brown wings deeply, 10–12 minutes total, in batches if needed. Wings deep golden brown, crisp at edges, leaving dark fond.
  3. Transfer wings to bowl with bacon; hold room temperature up to 30 minutes.

Phase 2 — Build and Reduce Base

  1. Add onion, celery, garlic, anchovy, and thyme; cook 3–5 minutes.
  2. Add wine, 1 cup / 240 ml broth, soy sauce, bay leaf, parsley stems, and lemon peel.
  3. Reduce aggressively 12–15 minutes. Vegetables begin lightly frying again in concentrated fat; liquid glossy and nearly evaporated.

Phase 3 — Build Stew

  1. Reduce heat to medium-low. Stir in butter.
  2. Add flour and cook 1 minute.
  3. Whisk in remaining broth.
  4. Add wings, bacon, potatoes, and first half carrots; bring to simmer.
  5. Transfer uncovered to oven for 30 minutes.

Phase 4 — Add Thighs

  1. Remove pot from oven and scrape fond above liquid line back into stew.
  2. Add thighs and remaining carrots.
  3. Return uncovered to oven 40–50 minutes. No pull temperature — thighs remain submerged. Paring knife slides through thickest thigh piece with no drag; thigh fibers separate cleanly under light pressure.

Holding & Assembly

  1. During final 30 minutes, combine gremolata in small uncovered bowl; discard and remake if held longer.
  2. Remove bay leaf, parsley stems, and lemon peel.
  3. Remove wings; reclaim meat from flats and drumettes. Discard bones, skin, and cartilage; return meat to stew.
  4. Stir in 1 tsp sherry vinegar; taste before adding the second teaspoon. Adjust salt and pepper.
  5. Hold uncovered off heat up to 20 minutes; loosen with hot broth if thickened.
  6. Serve with ~½ tsp gremolata per bowl.

Critical Control Points

SeverityFailure Mode & Sensory CueRecovery
CriticalIncomplete reduction — vegetables still floating in abundant liquid.Continue reduction until vegetables lightly fry.
MajorAdvancing with pale wings and weak fond — wings pale with minimal fond.Continue browning before aromatics.
MajorOver-thickened stew — coats spoon heavily.Thin with hot broth.
MajorOvercooked late carrots — carrots collapsing.Carrot texture is lost; stew remains serviceable.
MinorExcess gremolata — lemon/garlic dominate aroma.Reduce garnish.

Adjust: Adjust salt after vinegar; add second tsp vinegar only if stew reads heavy; thin with hot broth; increase gremolata slightly if richness dominates.

Orange Olive Oil Cake

Yield: one 9-inch cake / serves 8 · Total: 1 hr 30 min · Active: 20 min · Oven: 350°F / 177°C

A soft citrus olive oil cake that resets the meal with orange aroma and restrained sweetness.

Ingredients

IngredientAmount
Sugar200 g
Orange zestfrom 2 oranges
Eggs3
Extra-virgin olive oil180 ml / ¾ cup
Whole milk120 ml / ½ cup
Orange juice60 ml / ¼ cup
All-purpose flour210 g
Baking powder1½ tsp
Diamond Crystal kosher salt½ tsp
Powdered sugarfor finish

Prep

  1. Heat oven to 350°F / 177°C.
  2. Prepare 9-inch cake pan with neutral oil or olive oil; line base with parchment and lightly oil parchment.

Active Cooking

  1. Rub orange zest into sugar.
  2. Whisk in eggs.
  3. Add olive oil, milk, and orange juice.
  4. Fold in flour, baking powder, and salt. Fold only until dry flour disappears; stop before batter turns elastic.
  5. Bake 40–50 minutes. Skewer emerges with moist crumbs but no wet batter; center springs back lightly.

Holding & Assembly

  1. Cool 20 minutes before unmolding. If cake resists release, cool 10 minutes more.
  2. Hold uncovered at room temperature up to 8 hours.
  3. Dust lightly with powdered sugar before serving.

Critical Control Points

SeverityFailure Mode & Sensory CueRecovery
MajorUnderbaked center — wet batter on skewer.Continue baking in 5-minute increments.
MinorExcess mixing — batter becomes elastic.Fold minimally; stop as soon as flour disappears.

Adjust: Reduce powdered sugar if serving after wine; add fresh zest at service if more orange aroma is desired.
