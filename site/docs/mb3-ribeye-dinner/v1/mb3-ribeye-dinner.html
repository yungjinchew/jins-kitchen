Dish 1 — Reverse-Seared MB3+ Ribeye with Mushroom Finish

Yield: 3 · Total: ~1 hr 20 min · Active: ~25 min · Oven: 250°F / 120°C

Reverse sear establishes uniform internal doneness before the final high-heat crust; mushroom and parmesan finish served alongside, not over, the steak.

Ingredients

IngredientAmount
Ribeye steak, MB3+1.1 lb / 500 g
Diamond Crystal kosher saltTo season
Neutral oil15 ml / 1 tbsp
Frozen mushrooms200 g / 7 oz
Parmesan, finely grated15–20 g / 0.5–0.7 oz

Prep Phase

  1. Pat steak completely dry on all surfaces including edges and fat cap.
  2. Season with Diamond Crystal kosher salt on all surfaces.
  3. Leave uncovered at room temperature 45–60 min before entering the oven.

Active Cooking

Mushroom cook-off (Burner 2, T−13 to T−9) and steak sear (T−7 onward) are sequential in the same pan. Mushrooms must be fully cooked off, removed, and the pan wiped and re-oiled before the steak enters. Any residual moisture prevents crust formation.
  1. Place steak on rack over a tray; into oven at 250°F / 120°C.
  2. Check internal temp at T−23 (approx. 40 min). If approaching 105–108°F / 40–42°C, monitor every 3 min.
  3. Pull at 112–115°F / 44–46°C. Carries to 119–125°F during rest; final sear raises centre to approx. 125–130°F / 52–54°C. Do not rely on elapsed time alone.
  4. Remove to rack; rest uncovered. Begin sear at T−7.
  5. Mushroom cook-off (Burner 2, from T−13): dry pan at high heat, 4–5 min without excessive stirring. Primary cue: pan sounds dry — no sizzling water, only dry browning sounds. Remove mushrooms; wipe pan.
  6. Add 15 ml neutral oil to wiped pan; return to maximum heat. Oil must be visibly smoking before steak enters.
  7. Sear: 75–90 sec first side without moving. Release cue: steak lifts cleanly with deep even browning. Do not force the flip.
  8. Flip: 45–60 sec second side. Render fat cap on edge 20 sec.
  9. Remove steak to board. Return mushrooms to pan; add parmesan; 30–40 sec to gloss. Remove.

Secondary doneness cue (pre-sear): press centre lightly — if firm with significant resistance, shorten sear to 45–60 sec per side and skip the edge render.

Holding & Assembly

Rest on rack, uncovered. Begin sear at T−7 without delay.

Slice immediately before plating — do not slice and hold.

Spoon mushrooms alongside, not over, the steak. Serve steak partially over the bean braise.

Critical Control Points

SeverityFailure Mode & CueRecovery
CriticalReverse sear overshoot — steak feels firm at centre; edges show grey before sear beginsUnrecoverable — shorten sear to absolute minimum; serve promptly
MajorSear crust failure — pale grey surface on contact; pan moisture audible as weak sizzle instead of hard searUnrecoverable for that side; re-sear opposite side aggressively; accept uneven crust
MajorMushroom moisture in sear pan — steak sizzles weakly and steams on contactRemove steak; fully dry pan; re-heat oil to smoking; return steak
MinorParmesan dominates beef at service — cheese aroma stronger than beefReduce parmesan on mushrooms; do not apply directly to sliced steak

Adjust: Taste sliced steak before adding finish salt — MB3+ fat amplifies salt perception; adjust only if under-seasoned. If crust softens during the rest-to-sear window, a 20-sec reflash in a dry pan at maximum heat recovers it. If the plate reads heavy, increase braise proportion per bite rather than adding acid to the steak.

Dish 2 — Cannellini Bean, Tomato & Spinach Braise

Yield: 3 · Total: ~30 min · Active: ~15 min

Thick tomato-cannellini braise providing the meal’s acid balance — calibrated to support the steak, not compete with it.

Ingredients

IngredientAmount
Cannellini beans, 1 tin, drained and rinsed400 g / 14 oz
Canned tomatoes, crushed or chopped~300 g / ¾ of a 400 g tin
Frozen spinach120 g / 4 oz
Parmesan, finely grated20 g / 0.7 oz
Neutral or olive oil15 ml / 1 tbsp
Diamond Crystal kosher saltTo finish

Prep Phase

  1. Drain and rinse beans lightly. Some surface starch aids cohesion — do not rinse aggressively.

Active Cooking

  1. Heat oil over medium on Burner 1.
  2. Add ~300 g tomatoes. Reduce over medium, stirring occasionally, until reduced by roughly one-third and the surface coats a spoon lightly — approx. 15 min. If tomato flavour reads full and deep before this point, hold back the remaining ¼ tin.
  3. Add drained beans. Reduce to medium-low; simmer until cohesive and thick but beans hold their shape — approx. 15 min.
  4. Fold in frozen spinach; wilt fully, 2–3 min.
  5. Stir in parmesan off heat. Move to very low heat on Burner 1; cover and hold.

Holding & Assembly

Hold covered on very low heat on Burner 1 through service.

At T−3: stir and check consistency — loosen with 2–3 tbsp hot water if tightened.

Critical Control Points

SeverityFailure Mode & CueRecovery
MajorUnder-reduced — liquid runs freely off a spoon; no bodyContinue reducing uncovered; remove lid completely
MajorOver-reduced — paste-like texture; beans compacting and beginning to break downAdd hot water 2 tbsp at a time; stir over low heat
MinorSpinach metallic dominance — vegetal flavour obscures the tomato-bean baseSmall splash of reserved tomato liquid or extra tbsp parmesan

Adjust: Add salt only after reduction completes — it concentrates with the liquid. If the braise reads flat after the hold, add the reserved ¼ tin of tomatoes and stir briefly over low heat. Thin with water only — oil at this stage breaks the emulsification.

Dish 3 — Charred Gruyère Cauliflower

Yield: 3 · Total: ~20 min active · Equipment: induction Burner 2, butane torch

Pan-charred on induction at maximum heat, finished with butane-torched gruyère — provides bitterness, char, and texture contrast with no oven conflict.

Ingredients

IngredientAmount
Frozen cauliflower450 g / 1 lb
Gruyère, grated or thinly sliced40 g / 1.4 oz
Neutral oil15 ml / 1 tbsp
Diamond Crystal kosher saltTo season

Prep Phase

  1. Remove cauliflower from freezer at T−45; leave at room temperature to partially thaw.
  2. Pat dry at T−25 before placing in the pan.

Active Cooking

  1. At T−25: heat dry pan on Burner 2 to maximum heat. Cue: a drop of water evaporates in under 1 sec.
  2. Add cauliflower dry; do not overcrowd.
  3. Phase 1 — Moisture cook-off (~2–3 min): cauliflower will steam initially; wait until audible moisture fully subsides.
  4. Add 15 ml neutral oil; toss briefly.
  5. Phase 2 — Charring (~5–7 min): let florets sit in contact with the pan; turn only to expose uncharred surfaces. Primary cue: edges deeply charred, centres yield to gentle pressure but hold shape.
  6. Season lightly with Diamond Crystal.
  7. At T−13: transfer charred cauliflower to a heatproof dish (no cheese yet). Burner 2 is now free for mushrooms.
  8. At T−9: distribute gruyère evenly over cauliflower; butane torch in slow passes 5–8 cm above the cheese until fully melted, bubbling, and beginning to colour — 60–90 sec. Serve within 9 min.

Holding & Assembly

Serve uncovered within 9 min of torching. Do not cover. If hold extends, a brief 10-sec torch pass over the set cheese restores surface texture.

Critical Control Points

SeverityFailure Mode & CueRecovery
MajorPan too cool or overcrowded — pale wet surfaces after 4 min; no audible dry searReduce quantity; increase heat; press florets against surface with spatula
MajorOvercrowded pan — water pooling beneath florets; no contact browning visibleCook in 2 batches; first holds uncovered while second finishes
MinorGruyère burns under torch — dark, bitter-smelling spots on cheese surfaceMove torch faster and further away; underlying cheese typically intact

Adjust: Finish cauliflower lightly with salt after torching, before serving. If texture has softened during the hold, a brief 10-sec torch pass restores the crust. Reduce gruyère to 25 g / 0.9 oz if the meal reads heavy.

Dish 4 — Warm Blueberry-Strawberry Compote

Yield: 3 · Total: ~10 min · Active: ~5 min · Holds 30 min at room temperature

Warm, glossy berry compote serving as a light palate reset after the beef course — made at T−35, held at room temperature.

Ingredients

IngredientAmount
Frozen blueberries150 g / 5 oz
Frozen strawberries150 g / 5 oz
Diamond Crystal kosher saltOne pinch
SugarOptional — only if needed

Prep Phase

None required.

Active Cooking

  1. Add frozen berries to a small pan over low heat on Burner 2 at T−35.
  2. Warm gently until juices release and berries are fully thawed — approx. 5 min.
  3. Reduce lightly until surface is glossy but mixture remains fluid, not jammy.
  4. Add one pinch Diamond Crystal.
  5. Taste. Add sugar only if flavour reads flat or sharp. Remove from heat at T−25; hold at room temperature.

Holding & Assembly

Serve warm or at room temperature. Holds up to 30 min without quality loss.

Critical Control Points

SeverityFailure Mode & CueRecovery
MinorOver-reduced — jam-like body; skins wrinkling and separatingRemove from heat; add 2–3 tbsp water; stir briefly over low heat

Adjust: If the compote reads sweet and flat, increase the strawberry ratio or add a small squeeze of lime if available. Thin with water only if paste-like — do not reduce further once glossy.
